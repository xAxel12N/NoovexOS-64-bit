Realtek Wi-Fi firmware used by the rtw88 driver (RTL8821CE, RTL8822BE, RTL8822CE).

These files are unmodified copies from the linux-firmware project:
  rtw8821c_fw.bin   rtw88/rtw8821c_fw.bin
  rtw8822b_fw.bin   rtw88/rtw8822b_fw.bin
  rtw8822c_fw.bin   rtw88/rtw8822c_fw.bin

Realtek Bluetooth firmware (linux-firmware btusb entry, same LICENCE.rtlwifi_firmware.txt):
  rtl_bt/rtl8821c_fw.bin, rtl_bt/rtl8821c_config.bin, rtl_bt/rtl8822cu_fw.bin, rtl_bt/rtl8761bu_config.bin
  (rtl8761bu_config.bin is used as rtl8822cu_config.bin; see rtl_bt/WHENCE.txt)

Corporation, under the licence in LICENCE.rtlwifi_firmware.txt (next to this file, and
